Current MPhil research

Natural-language interfaces for semantic building data

Grounding questions in building knowledge graphs before planning analysis over linked time series.

Research overview

Ground first, analyse second

Problem and approach

Bryan’s MPhil research explores staged natural-language interfaces for building data.

The approach first grounds a question in Brick knowledge graphs and validates the selected equipment, spaces, points, and observed graph paths; only then does it plan analysis over linked time series.

Motivation

By exposing graph evidence and temporal plans before calculation—and clarifying or declining when evidence is insufficient—the work aims to make failures easier to diagnose and answers more reliable.

Current status

Current work includes a reproducible KGQA prototype and a preliminary feasibility study; broader BuildingQA and time-series evaluations remain in progress.
